## SPIDER

The supplied https://zenodo.org/records/10159224 is v2. Its latest-version link resolves to **v4, record 10159290**, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10159290. Official metadata confirms **218 studies / 218 patients and 447 sagittal T1/T2 series**, vertebral/disc/canal segmentations, official training/validation assignments in overview.csv, and a hidden challenge test set. Disc narrowing is listed; canal stenosis grading must not be inferred from it.

v4 lists images.zip (3.7 GB, MD5 7a9fa44aac0c72e2937bdef62cff88db), masks.zip (58.2 MB, MD5 49861ff25a0108b3b69d0ce808ae51dc), overview.csv and radiological_gradings.csv. Archives downloaded and checked against official MD5 values; see the integrity report. Plan additional space for extraction, preprocessed caches, checkpoints and experiment logs before training; 114 GiB free is not a guarantee that all experiments fit.

The Grand Challenge data page returned HTTP 403 and the rendered Zenodo license field was empty in the web tool. A direct request to the **official API https://zenodo.org/api/records/10159290** resolved this: `metadata.license.id` is **cc-by-4.0**. Preserve attribution to Jasper Willem van der Graaf and coauthors, the v4 DOI, and https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03090-w . Native format is MHA. Image archive 3,700,562,886 bytes, expanded 3,707,013,638 bytes; masks archive 58,222,897 bytes, expanded 87,032,277 bytes. Research image files do not validate original DICOM ingestion.
